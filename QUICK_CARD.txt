STEP 1: OPEN TERMINAL IN VS CODE

After opening schooloo.ai-main in VS Code:

Press:  Ctrl + ` (Control + Backtick key)

You'll see a terminal appear at the bottom of VS Code.

STEP 2: RUN THE STARTUP COMMAND
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Copy & paste this command:

    bash start_local.sh

Press: Enter

Wait for: "🎉 Application is ready!" message
Expected wait time: 5-10 seconds

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3: OPEN IN BROWSER & USE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Open your browser and go to:

    http://localhost:8000

Or click the link that appears in the terminal output.

You should see the Schooloo AI chatbot. Start typing your questions!

STOPPING THE APP
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

In the terminal where you ran the command:

Press: Ctrl + C

The servers will stop.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Copy & paste this to force restart:

    pkill -9 -f "python3" || true && sleep 2 && bash start_local.sh

This will:
1. Stop any running servers
2. Wait 2 seconds  
3. Restart everything fresh
